## Venue criteria binding

Scientific Reports publishes original research across engineering and applied science and expects methods and experimental data sufficient for reproducibility. Venue-fit assessment is bound to the official [Aims & Scope](https://www.nature.com/srep/about/aims), [Editorial Policies](https://www.nature.com/srep/journal-policies/editorial-policies), and [Submission Guidelines](https://www.nature.com/srep/author-instructions/submission-guidelines). The review separates broad scope fit from scientific validity and submission readiness.
